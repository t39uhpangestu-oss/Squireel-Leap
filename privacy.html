Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 29, 2026

Squirrel Leap 2: Endless Jump ("the Game") is a free browser game. This page explains what information the Game handles.

Information we collect

We do not collect, store, or transmit personal information. The Game does not require an account, does not ask for your name, email address, or location, and does not use cookies for tracking.

Information stored on your device

The Game saves your best score in your browser's local storage so it remains available the next time you play. This data stays on your device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. You can delete it at any time by clearing your browser's site data.

Offline use

The Game uses a service worker to cache its files so it can run offline after the first visit. Caching only stores the Game's own files on your device.

Third parties

The Game does not include advertising, analytics, or third-party tracking services. It makes no external network requests while being played.

Children

The Game does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.
